An independent, decision-only fact-checking model — a local Jev replacement.

4B + LoRAall 3 primitives runs locallyzero marginal cost

Send a claim plus the evidence retrieved for it, ask typed questions whose answers are declared in the request, and get back a calibrated probability distribution — one forward pass, no text generation, no parsing. The label set and each label's natural-language definition arrive at call time, so a differently-worded question or a different number of labels works without retraining.

Reliability curve

Both models are overconfident between 0.5 and 0.8 stated confidence. They diverge most at the low-confidence end: where ours states ~0.38 it is right ~0.38 of the time, while Jev states ~0.38 and is right ~0.21. Low confidence is the range a system uses to decide whether to act, so that is the place where honest uncertainty matters most.

Try it

curl -s http://127.0.0.1:8078/v1/systemone \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
    "state": {"claim": "The Eiffel Tower is in Paris.",
              "evidence": [{"title": "Eiffel Tower",
                            "text": "A wrought-iron tower on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France."}]},
    "questions": {
      "verdict": {"type": "choice",
                  "instructions": "Does the evidence establish the claim?",
                  "criteria": {"supported": "The evidence entails the claim.",
                               "refuted": "The evidence contradicts it.",
                               "not_enough_info": "The evidence cannot decide it."}},
      "has_date": {"type": "noul", "instructions": "Does the evidence contain a date or number?"}
    }
  }'

What it is

Classification is read out of a real language model's own next-token distribution over the declared labels — no classification head is bolted on, and nothing is generated. That keeps it text-conditioned: labels are defined in language at call time, the way Jev's are, rather than fixed by training.

Measured against TypeSafe Jev 1.13.0 on identical rows. Jev is a hosted commercial model; this one is 4B + a 4.4M-parameter LoRA adapter, and it runs on a laptop GPU.
